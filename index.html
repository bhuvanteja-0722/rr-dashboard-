Methodology, in brief

Declared thresholds; player-versus-self baselines; exposure and consistency shrinkage.

Pressure state

Chase rate at least 9/over; late chase at least 7.5/over in the final 30 balls with six or fewer wickets; late squeeze, collapse recovery, or final 10 overs of a knockout.

Batter signal

75% standardized strike-rate improvement plus 25% standardized dismissal-rate improvement against the same player’s ordinary innings.

Bowler signal

60% standardized economy improvement plus 40% improvement in credited wickets per six legal balls, against the same player’s ordinary overs.

Controls: minimum pressure and ordinary balls, minimum effective pressure matches, season-format baselines, recency weighting, sample shrinkage, and a Beta-shrunk consistency term. Final consensus is mean rank across Core, Narrow and Broad pressure definitions; candidates qualify under at least two. Sample confidence and rule sensitivity should guide review.
